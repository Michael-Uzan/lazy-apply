Senior Embedded Firmware Engineer — Medical Devices
Caliper Medical · Haifa (On-site)

About the role
You will develop firmware for Class III implantable medical devices, working
close to the silicon and to the regulatory process.

Requirements
- 5+ years of embedded C/C++ firmware development
- Deep experience with ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers and RTOS
- Hardware bring-up, schematic reading, oscilloscope and logic analyser work
- IEC 62304 / FDA Class III regulatory experience
- DO-178C or equivalent safety-critical software lifecycle background
- B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering or Computer Engineering

Nice to have
- Experience with DSP and signal filtering on constrained hardware
- Familiarity with wireless protocols for medical telemetry (BLE, MICS)
