- Hands-on with Node.js services in production
- Comfortable with PostgreSQL and data modelling
- Experience with real-time data in the browser (WebSockets or similar)
- Track record of measurable performance work

Nice to have
- Experience in fintech or trading systems
- Exposure to event-driven architectures and message queues
- Mentoring or tech-lead experience
